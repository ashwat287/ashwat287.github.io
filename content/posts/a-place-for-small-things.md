There’s something nice about having a small place of your own on the internet. A place where an idea doesn’t need to become a polished essay before it is worth sharing.

This is a sample entry for that kind of place: a notebook for software, experiments, and things learned along the way.

## What belongs here?

- A bug that took longer to understand than to fix.
- A small tool that made a repeated task easier.
- Notes from learning a new language or reading some interesting code.
- A project that is still a work in progress.

Not every post needs to be a tutorial. Sometimes a clear account of what happened, what you tried, and what you learned is enough.

## Start with one note

Replace this sample with something you’ve been thinking about. Keep it short if that helps. The useful part is getting the idea onto the page.
